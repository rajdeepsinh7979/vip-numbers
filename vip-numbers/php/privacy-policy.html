Shipping & Delivery Policy

Since this website deals with mobile/SIM number porting rather than physical product shipping, the delivery process is electronic/activation-based.

1. Porting Code Delivery

Within 24 hours of receiving the payment, we will send the porting code for the selected mobile number to the customer through the communication method used for the order.

The porting code will be valid for 4 days.

The customer must use the porting code within its validity period to proceed with the activation/porting process.

2. SIM Activation Process

After receiving the porting code:

  1. Visit your nearest mobile/SIM service store.
  2. Take the provided porting code with you.
  3. Provide your Aadhaar Card or other valid government-issued ID proof as required for the activation/KYC process.
  4. Complete the required activation process at the mobile store.
  5. The shopkeeper/service provider will provide the SIM card after collecting the applicable SIM card charges and recharge amount directly from the customer.

3. Activation Time

After completing the required activation process at the mobile store, the SIM is expected to be activated within 7 working days.

Activation time may vary depending on the telecom operator, KYC verification, porting process, or other technical/regulatory requirements.

4. Customer Responsibility

The customer is responsible for:

  • Using the porting code before its 4-day validity expires.
  • Visiting the nearest mobile store for activation.
  • Providing valid Aadhaar or other government-approved ID proof as required.
  • Completing the required KYC/activation process.
  • Paying the applicable SIM card and recharge charges directly at the mobile store.
  • Providing accurate information during the activation process.
